# COSHIAN Music License (CML)

CML is a separate license intended for music and music-related works. It is under development; its terms, approval, and publication have not been finalized. No authoritative license text is available here. CDL conditions do not automatically apply to CML.

Japanese will be authoritative. English translations are unofficial references unless explicitly approved by the owner. The proposed custom SPDX identifier `LicenseRef-CML-1.0` is reserved for possible future use; it is not an officially registered SPDX identifier and does not mean CML v1.0 exists.

See the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md), [FAQ](FAQ.md), [drafts](drafts/README.md), and [versions](versions/README.md). CML versions are managed independently of CDL. Published authoritative texts must not be changed in place; substantive changes require a new version.
