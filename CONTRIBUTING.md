# Contributing

Use the license question issue form for questions and the amendment proposal form for proposed changes. Describe typographical corrections, translation corrections, and ambiguous or contradictory provisions in an issue or pull request, identifying the license, version, articles, and relevant text. Do not include confidential information.

Changes to drafts, translations, and documentation are proposed on `develop`. Explain the purpose and verification in the pull request. The repository owner reviews proposals and decides whether to approve them; a pull request is a proposal and does not automatically become an official amendment. Legal review or publication must not be presumed from a submission or merge.

Preserve published versions: never modify a published authoritative text in place, even for typographical corrections. Report the issue and propose a new version where a change to that text is needed. Substantive amendments require a new version. Check translation corrections against the authoritative Japanese text and identify any change in meaning.

Submitting a contribution does not by itself transfer copyright ownership. Any additional contributor licensing terms require explicit owner approval; no such terms are established here.
