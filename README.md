# COSHIAN Licenses

This repository centrally manages the COSHIAN Develop License (CDL) and COSHIAN Music License (CML) as independent licenses.

- [CDL](CDL/README.md) is intended for development works. The [Japanese v1.0 text](CDL/versions/1.0/LICENSE-ja.md) and an unofficial English reference translation are available for review; approval and publication status are unconfirmed.
- [CML](CML/README.md) is intended for music and music-related works and is under development. Its terms have not been finalized.

No license version is identified here as approved or published. `main` is intended for approved, published versions; `develop` is for drafts, translations, documentation, and proposed amendments. See the [CDL version information](CDL/README.md) and [CML versions](CML/versions/README.md).

Japanese license texts are authoritative. English translations are unofficial references unless explicitly approved by the repository owner. For CDL v1.0, Section 12.6 gives the official Japanese text precedence over an unofficial translation to the extent not contrary to applicable law.

To apply a license, select a specific version that the owner has approved and published, review its authoritative text, and explicitly identify that license and version with the work. No version is available for application from this repository at present.

Questions, corrections, and amendments are welcome through the procedures in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Proposed changes require owner review and approval.

Future tags may use `CDL-v1.0`, `CDL-v1.1`, `CML-v1.0`, and `CML-v1.1`; drafts may use names such as `CDL-v1.1-draft` or `CML-v0.3-draft`. Versions are managed independently. Published authoritative texts are not modified in place; substantive changes require a new version.

`LicenseRef-CDL-1.0` and `LicenseRef-CML-1.0` are proposed custom SPDX LicenseRef identifiers, not officially registered SPDX license identifiers. The CML identifier is reserved for possible future use and does not mean CML v1.0 exists.
