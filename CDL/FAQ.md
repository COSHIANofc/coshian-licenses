# CDL FAQ

## Is CDL v1.0 officially published?

Yes. CDL v1.0 was officially published on November 25, 2026, as an [immutable GitHub Release](https://github.com/COSHIANofc/coshian-licenses/releases/tag/CDL-v1.0).

## Can CDL v1.0 be applied to a project?

Yes. A project owner with sufficient rights can explicitly apply it to eligible works. Review the authoritative text and see the [application instructions](../README.md#applying-cdl-v10).

## Where can I obtain the official license documents?

The [CDL v1.0 release](https://github.com/COSHIANofc/coshian-licenses/releases/tag/CDL-v1.0) provides Japanese and English texts in Markdown and plain text. Links to all four assets are in the [repository README](../README.md#cdl-v10-documents).

## Which language version is authoritative?

The Japanese text is authoritative. The English translation is accepted for distribution as an unofficial reference. Section 12.6 provides that the Japanese text controls over an unofficial translation to the extent not contrary to applicable law.

## What does CDL v1.0 cover?

Section 1.2 covers specified development-related works when CDL is explicitly applied. See the [Japanese text](versions/1.0/LICENSE-ja.md) for its full scope and conditions.

## Can works be used commercially?

Sections 2.2 and 4.1 permit commercial use, subject to the license conditions. Distribution is subject to Articles 5 and 6.

## What happens when modified files are distributed?

Section 5.5 requires the same CDL version for specified covered source files and directly modified covered materials, subject to the qualifications in that section. Section 5.6 addresses independent works. Article 6 addresses corresponding source for distribution in executable form. Read the full text for all conditions and exceptions. Compatibility with other licenses has not been formally established.

## Is CDL OSI-approved?

No. CDL is not OSI-approved.

## Is the custom SPDX identifier officially registered?

No. `LicenseRef-CDL-1.0` is a custom LicenseRef, not an officially registered SPDX license identifier.

## Has independent legal review been completed?

No. Independent legal review is deferred and was not required for this release.

## Is CML available?

No published CML version is available. CML is a separate license under development; its terms are not finalized. See [CML](../CML/README.md).
