# CDL FAQ

## What does CDL v1.0 cover?

Section 1.2 covers specified development-related works when CDL is explicitly applied. See the [Japanese text](versions/1.0/LICENSE-ja.md) for its full scope and conditions.

## Can works be used commercially?

Sections 2.2 and 4.1 permit commercial use, subject to the license conditions. Distribution is subject to Articles 5 and 6.

## What happens when modified files are distributed?

Section 5.5 requires the same CDL version for specified covered source files and directly modified covered materials, subject to the qualifications in that section. Section 5.6 addresses independent works. Article 6 addresses corresponding source for distribution in executable form. Read the full text for all conditions and exceptions.

## Is an English translation authoritative?

The [English translation](versions/1.0/LICENSE-en.md) is an unofficial reference. Section 12.6 provides that the official Japanese text controls over an unofficial translation to the extent not contrary to applicable law.

## Is CDL approved by OSI or registered with SPDX?

No such approval or registration is claimed. `LicenseRef-CDL-1.0` is a custom LicenseRef identifier.
